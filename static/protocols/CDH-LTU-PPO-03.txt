 11. ADDITIONAL CLINICAL ORDERS
 Blood Glucose                   ☐ Blood glucose q4h initially. Insulin sliding scale per ICU protocol. Target glucose 6–10
                                 mmol/L (steroid-induced hyperglycaemia common post-transplant).
 Electrolytes                    ☐ Replace Mg and Phos to normal range. Tacrolimus causes Mg wasting — repletion is
                                 critical. Replace K to maintain K ≥4.0 mmol/L.
